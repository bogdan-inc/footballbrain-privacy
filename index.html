Football Brain – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Football Brain (the “App”, package app.footballbrain), operated by Swisstalk.app (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and shares your personal data. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple / Google if you use those options; display name, username, language, and profile details you choose to add (for example position, club, country, age group, profile photo).
  • Content you create: game and training results, scores, streaks, messages in AI chats, transfer-hub applications and notes you submit.
  • Photos: only if you choose to upload a profile picture; the App then accesses the single photo you select (via camera or photo library).
  • Purchase data: subscription status and purchase identifiers. Payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details.
  • Device and technical data: push notification token (if you allow notifications), app version, device model and operating system, and basic diagnostic information.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers, and we do not show third-party ads.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and provide the App’s features.
  • To generate AI coaching responses to your messages.
  • To manage subscriptions and unlock Premium features.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent abuse and fix bugs.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Service providers who process data for us

  • Supabase – database, authentication and server functions (hosting of account and app data).
  • RevenueCat – subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – payment processing and sign-in.
  • OpenAI – the text of your AI chat messages is sent to OpenAI to generate replies. Do not include sensitive personal information in chat messages.
  • Expo / Apple / Google push services – delivery of notifications.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers above, when required by law, or with your consent.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the App: My Profile → Settings → Delete account. Data is removed from our database immediately; backups are purged within a reasonable period. You may also request deletion by emailing us (see section 8).

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and international transfers

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with reputable providers. Your data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States and the EU, with appropriate safeguards.

Questions or requests: swisstalk11@gmail.com

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced in the App.
